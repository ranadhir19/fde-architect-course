# Capstone 5 — The take-home simulation (graduation)

A timed simulation of a real Forward Deployed Engineer take-home, modelled on
documented hiring processes at the labs: roughly one week of calendar time, a
few focused hours of build time, graded on customer framing, the decision log,
and a small eval set — where a documented 70% beats an undocumented 100%.

**The brief** is released when you start your attempt (it rotates; the current
brief lives in this folder when your cohort window opens). The shape never
changes: an ambiguous customer problem, data you must interrogate before you
model, and a system a real user could touch.

**Change injection.** Midway through the attempt window, one constraint in the
brief tightens for everyone at the same moment — a deadline moves, or a data
source is withdrawn. How you re-plan is part of the grade.

**Deliverables.** Working code; a running app; `DECISIONS.md` that shows your
framing, your cuts, and your trade-offs; a small eval set with results; a
rollout sketch; and a recorded walkthrough of at most 5 minutes.

**Self-scoring.** Score yourself against the published rubric before you watch
the reference walkthrough. The gap between your score and the reference is the
last lesson of the course.

**Rubric.** Customer framing 25% · decision log 25% · working system 25% ·
eval set 15% · walkthrough 10%.

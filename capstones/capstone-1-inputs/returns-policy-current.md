# Cedarbrook Home Goods — Returns policy (as published, engagement start)

Fictional policy document, provided for Capstone 1. This is the
text on the customer's public policy page on day one of the
engagement. What agents actually honour on the phone may differ;
discovering that difference is part of the engagement (see
FDE 1.03 and FDE 1.08).

## The policy as published

- **Window.** Items may be returned within **30 days** of
  delivery, for any reason, provided they are unused and in
  their original packaging.
- **Damaged on arrival.** Report within 7 days of delivery for
  a replacement or refund. One category covers all damage.
- **Process.** Contact support for a returns label; refunds are
  issued to the original payment method within 10 working days
  of the returned item reaching the warehouse.
- **Exchanges.** Treated as a return plus a new order.
- **Exceptions.** Clearance items are final sale. Mattresses
  are excluded for hygiene reasons.

## Known gaps (stated by the customer in the brief)

- The policy was last rewritten three years ago.
- The operations manager owns this document; changes need her
  signature.
- Nothing on this page describes the walk-up ("hard ones")

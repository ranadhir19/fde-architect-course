# Capstone 1 — Retail support, messy data

**The customer.** A mid-size online retailer. Support volume is up 40% year on
year; the team lead's ask is "automate support." Nobody has measured anything.

**The brief.** Shadow the (provided) ticket history, find the workflow actually
worth automating, scope a v1, and build a triage system that drafts responses
for the top categories — with a human in the loop.

**Provided inputs.** 2,000 historical tickets (inconsistent categories, three
merged legacy taxonomies, duplicates, silent empty fields), the current
returns policy, and a spreadsheet the support lead actually runs the rota from.

**Change injection (week 2).** The returns policy changes: two categories your
system handles are redefined. Your eval set, your drafts, and your rollout plan
must absorb it — and your DECISIONS.md must show the moment it landed.

**Deliverables.** Discovery notes; a falsifiable value hypothesis; the scoped
v1 with written reasons for every cut; the running triage system; a 50-case
eval set drawn from real tickets; a rollout plan (shadow → canary → scale).

**Rubric.** Framing 25% · scope discipline 20% · system quality 25% ·
eval quality 20% · decision log 10%.

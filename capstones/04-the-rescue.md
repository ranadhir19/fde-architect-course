# Capstone 4 — The rescue

**The customer.** A manufacturer whose AI deployment — built by a previous
vendor — is live, drifting, and distrusted. Users route around it. The sponsor
who bought it has left. You inherit the system, its history, and the room's
scepticism.

**The brief.** Audit before you touch. Decide repair vs rebuild with evidence,
execute the call, and win back a user base that has already decided.

**Provided inputs.** The broken system (this repo ships it: undocumented
prompts, a stale eval set, silent data drift, one integration held together by
a cron job nobody owns), its incident history, and user complaints.

**Change injection.** During your audit, the system fails visibly in front of
the customer's leadership. Your forensic timeline just became a live
communication problem.

**Deliverables.** The forensic audit memo (what is broken, what is merely
unloved, what is fine); the repair-vs-rebuild decision with costed options;
the executed fix; a re-launch plan with adoption leading indicators; the memo
to leadership that tells the truth without burying the predecessor.

**Rubric.** Audit accuracy 30% · decision quality 25% · execution 25% ·
communication 20%.

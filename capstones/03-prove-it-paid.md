# Capstone 3 — Prove it paid

**The customer.** A logistics operator. The system from the brief — a
document-processing assistant for freight paperwork — is already built and
deployed to one depot. The CFO's question is the whole engagement: *did it
pay?*

**The brief.** Design the measurement before touching the dashboard: baseline,
canary cohort with a control, and an ROI model that survives a sceptical
finance review.

**Provided inputs.** Six months of pre-deployment processing data, three months
of post-deployment data with known confounders (a volume surge, a staffing
change, a holiday peak), and the vendor cost ledger.

**Change injection.** Halfway through, the operator expands to a second depot
with different paperwork. Your measurement design must say what transfers and
what must be re-baselined — before the expansion contaminates your control.

**Deliverables.** The measurement design; the computed ROI with uncertainty
stated; the one-pager for the CFO; and — if the honest number is negative —
the report that says so, with the recommendation that follows from it.

**Rubric.** Design soundness 30% · statistical honesty 30% · communication 25% ·
decision log 15%.

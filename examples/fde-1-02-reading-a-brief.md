# Worked example — FDE 1.02: reading a brief for the number

Referenced by the FDE 1.02 learning session. The brief is fictional.

A warehousing firm writes: "Our dispatchers are overwhelmed; we want an AI
assistant." Reading for the number instead of the ask: dispatchers touch 340
loads a day; a load booked to the wrong vehicle costs a $450 rebooking fee
and happens about 9 times a week — about $210k a year, countable today, and
the dispatch manager's bonus already rides on rebookings. The workflow worth
framing is not "assist dispatchers"; it is vehicle-load matching with the
rebooking rate as the metric. That is the move FDE 1.02 teaches: find the
number someone is paid to move, then find the workflow that moves it.

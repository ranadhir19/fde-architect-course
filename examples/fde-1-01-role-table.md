# Worked example — FDE 1.01: the five-role table, worked

Referenced by the FDE 1.01 learning and whiteboard sessions.

| Role | When | Builds for | Measured on | Leaves when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales engineer | Before the sale | The standard product | Winning the evaluation | The contract signs |
| Solutions architect | Design phase | A blueprint | The design being sound | The design is handed over |
| Consultant | Advisory engagement | A recommendation | The advice being right | The report lands |
| Product engineer | Always | Every customer at once | The roadmap shipping | Never — owns the product |
| **Forward Deployed Engineer** | **After the sale** | **One customer, in their systems** | **Adoption and the metric moving** | **The workflow runs without them** |

Worked call: "Set up a proof of concept on the prospect's data so their VP
can decide." It feels like deployment, but it is pre-sale and disposable —
sales engineering. The same build, repeated after signature inside the
customer's VPC with an adoption target, is FDE work. Timing and
accountability, not the code, decide the role.

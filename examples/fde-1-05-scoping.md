# Worked example — FDE 1.05: scoping by saying no, annotated

Referenced by the FDE 1.05 learning session. The customer is fictional;
the scoping is a composite written for this course. Annotations in
brackets name the move being made. This example uses a different
customer from the exercise, on purpose: copy the moves, not the words.

> **The room:** a fictional regional courier firm, Larkspur Freight
> (from the FDE 1.04 example). The operations director arrived with
> nine asks, a six-week window, and one engineer — the FDE.
>
> *[A wish list is a record of pain, not a plan. Every item on it is
> evidence about where the work hurts; treating it as a queue to be
> worked top to bottom is the failure this topic exists to prevent.]*
>
> **What got scoped as v1 (two items only):** generate the morning
> route board in depot gate-time order, and time the board-building
> work for two weeks so the FDE 1.04 hypothesis has its falsifier
> data. Everything else waited — including the director's own first
> ask, an AI dispatcher.
>
> *[v1 size is set by the hypothesis test, not by the window's
> apparent capacity. Two items that test the bet beat nine items
> that spread the same hours across every stakeholder's favourite.]*
>
> **The hardest cut, said in the room:** "The dispatcher is the
> thing you actually want, and I am asking you not to build it yet —
> because if board-building is not the cost, the dispatcher automates
> the wrong morning. Give the test two weeks; if the hour comes
> back, we scope the dispatcher on evidence, and I will write that
> scope with you."
>
> *[Saying no works when it is a deferral with a condition and a
> date, offered by the person who will do the deferred work — not a
> refusal, and never a quiet drop. The stakeholder hears what
> happens instead, and when.]*
>
> **The lesson:** scope is the hypothesis defended against the room.
> A v1 that pleases everyone in the scoping meeting tests nothing,
> and an engagement that tests nothing has no number to land —
> only a list of things that got built.

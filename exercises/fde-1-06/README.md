# FDE 1.06 Exercise — A plan and a risk register for a messy brief

**Topic:** Delivery planning under ambiguity (Layer 2). **Part (d) of FDE 1.06.**
Attempt this before watching the tutorial half of the lesson.

## The task

Read `messy-brief.md`. It holds the state of the Cedarbrook Home
Goods engagement (the fictional Capstone 1 customer) the morning
after scoping: the FDE 1.05 v1 is agreed, and the brief that arrives
to plan it is contradictory, partly verbal, and missing dates its
own deadlines depend on. That is the normal condition of a delivery
plan, not an exception to it.

Write `delivery-plan.md` from `delivery-plan-template.md`. It must
contain:

- A milestone plan for the three remaining weeks, where every
  milestone is tied to a value slice from the FDE 1.05 v1 — not to
  an activity. "Integration work continues" is not a milestone;
  "agents answer returns questions from one checked path, on live
  tickets, for one full day" is.
- A weekly demo cadence: who sees what, on which day, and what
  decision each demo exists to force. A demo with no decision
  attached is a status meeting wearing a costume.
- A risk register of at least six risks, each with a named owner,
  an early-warning signal you can actually observe, and a response
  that is decided now — not "monitor closely".
- An ambiguity log: every contradiction or missing fact in the
  brief, what you assumed in order to plan anyway, who can settle
  it, and by when the plan breaks if nobody does.

## Done means

- Every milestone names the value slice it delivers and the
  evidence that will show it landed. A milestone whose evidence is
  "work completed" scores zero.
- No milestone depends on a person, date, or signature the brief
  says is unavailable, without the register naming that dependency
  as a risk with a decided response.
- The plan still delivers the FDE 1.04 falsifier check on time
  even if the single most likely risk fires. If it cannot, the
  plan says so and re-sequences — it does not hope.
- Graded with `RUBRIC.md`. `SOLUTION.md` is the reference answer,
  released after the attempt window.

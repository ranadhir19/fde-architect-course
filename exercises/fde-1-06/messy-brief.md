# Provided input — the messy brief (fictional)

**Cedarbrook Home Goods** is the fictional Capstone 1 customer from
FDE 1.02 to FDE 1.05: a mid-size online retailer, about 180 staff,
support run from a single office. Everything below is invented for
this course. Treat anything not stated as unknown, and treat every
contradiction below as real — do not silently pick the version you
prefer.

## Where the engagement stands

FDE 1.05 scoped a four-item v1, in ship order: (1) reconcile and
publish one signed returns answer; (2) one checked answer path for
returns questions in the tool agents already use; (3) log the
walk-up queue in the ticket system; (4) the measurement slice only
of the category fix, for the falsifier sample weeks. The FDE 1.04
value hypothesis is tested by a falsifier checkable four weeks
after the reconciled answer ships. The sponsor needs a number for
the board seven weeks after discovery ended. Three weeks of
engagement time remain before that board date — which means the
falsifier's four-week clock must start inside the first week of
the plan, or the board number cannot come from the test.

## The brief, as it arrived

- The sponsor's email says the board meets "at the end of the
  month" and, two paragraphs later, "in five weeks". The support
  lead says the board date was moved once already and may move
  again. No written board date exists.
- The operations manager — whose signature item 1 needs — is on
  leave for the first four days of week one and has told the
  support lead she will sign "the returns thing" when she returns.
  She has not seen the reconciled draft. The draft does not exist
  yet: reconciling it is item 1.
- The support lead, your half-time counterpart, is committed to a
  peak-season rota in week three and estimates she will have "a
  few hours, maybe" that week. She is the only person who can
  confirm the checked answer path matches how agents actually work.
- The senior agent whose walk-up queue item 3 logs has agreed in
  principle and asked, reasonably, that logging take her no extra
  time per ticket. Nobody has timed the logging step.
- The ticket tool allows one configuration change window per week,
  on Wednesdays, booked a week ahead. Nobody has booked one.
- Finance's cost-per-ticket work can start in week two at the
  earliest. The sponsor has begun telling colleagues the board
  number will be "the saving", although the hypothesis is about
  handling effort, not dollars, and no cost figure exists.
- Ticket volume is entering the seasonal peak. The hand-counted
  baseline week was not a peak week. Nobody knows the peak uplift.

## What planning must protect

The board number comes from the FDE 1.04 test or it does not come
at all. A plan that protects every stakeholder's feelings and lets
the falsifier clock start late has failed at the one job it had.

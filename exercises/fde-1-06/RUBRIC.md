# FDE 1.06 Rubric (24 points)

- Milestones as value slices (6): every milestone names the v1
  slice it delivers and checkable evidence it landed. An activity
  milestone ("draft the answer", "hold the workshop") earns none
  for that week; a week whose milestone protects the falsifier
  clock starting in week one earns full marks only if the clock's
  start is the milestone's explicit point.
- Demo cadence (4): each weekly demo names its audience, what is
  shown, and the decision it forces. A demo with no decision, or a
  cadence that demos to the sponsor only and never to the agents
  who must adopt the change, loses two.
- Risk register (6): at least six risks, each with a named owner,
  an observable early-warning signal, and a response decided now.
  The signature risk, the week-three counterpart risk, and the
  Wednesday change-window risk must all appear; a missing one
  loses two. "Monitor closely" earns none for that risk.
- Ambiguity log (4): the board-date contradiction, the unseen
  signature draft, and the untimed logging step are each logged
  with an assumption, a settler, and an expiry. Silently choosing
  one board date with no log entry loses all four.
- Falsifier protection (4): the plan still lands the falsifier
  check on time if the single most likely risk fires, or it
  re-sequences openly and says what slips instead. A plan that
  protects the board number by relabelling effort as dollars —
  the sponsor's "the saving" — earns none and is flagged.
- Automatic fail: any milestone depends on the operations manager
  during her leave with no register entry; the plan promises a
  dollar saving to the board.
- Pass: 18 of 24 and no automatic fail.

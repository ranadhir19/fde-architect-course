# FDE 1.01 Rubric

- Classification accuracy: 12 points, one per task (tests).
- Defence quality: 12 points — a defence earns its point only if it names the
  deciding fact, not a restatement of the role name.
- Pass: 20 of 24, with no more than one FDE task misclassified — confusing
  the FDE boundary is the exact error this topic exists to remove.

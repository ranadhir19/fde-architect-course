# FDE 1.05 Rubric (24 points)

- v1 discipline (6): exactly four items, sequenced in ship order,
  each tied to the value hypothesis or a named prerequisite for
  testing it. A fifth item in any disguise, or a v1 item with no
  hypothesis tie, loses all six.
- Cut reasons (6): all ten cuts carry a criterion (value, evidence,
  dependency, ownership, or risk) a reader can argue with, plus a
  reconsider condition. "Phase two" or "out of scope" as the whole
  reason earns none for that item.
- Constraint use (4): the cuts actually use the stated constraints —
  the eight-week vendor lead time rules out the warehouse integration
  in this window, procurement rules out the tool replacement, and
  the three-week clock rules out anything that delays the hypothesis
  test. A scope that ignores a stated constraint loses two per item.
- Pushback paragraph (4): sendable as-is; acknowledges the ask,
  states the cut and criterion without blame, and names what happens
  instead with a reconsider point. A paragraph that promises the
  item "soon" without a condition earns none.
- Cannot-succeed call (4): names the single blocking condition (the
  operations manager's signature on one reconciled answer) and a
  concrete alternative recommendation. Naming a generic risk
  ("stakeholder alignment") earns none.
- Automatic fail: v1 includes the storefront chatbot or the ticket
  tool replacement; any cut reason that misstates a constraint
  (for example, claiming the warehouse integration was refused
  rather than lead-time blocked).
- Pass: 18 of 24 and no automatic fail.

# Provided brief — Capstone 1 customer (fictional)

**Cedarbrook Home Goods** is a fictional mid-size online retailer:
homeware and small furniture, about 180 staff, one warehouse, support
run from a single office. Facts the brief gives you — treat everything
not stated as unknown:

- Support volume is up 40% year on year. Nobody has measured why.
- The support lead's ask, verbatim: "automate support."
- Tickets arrive by email, web form, and phone notes typed up later.
  Categories were merged from three legacy systems two years ago; agents
  say the categories "do not match what people actually ask about".
- The returns policy is owned by the operations manager and was last
  rewritten three years ago.
- The support lead keeps the rota and the category counts in a personal
  spreadsheet. It is the only reporting the team has.
- One senior agent, nine years on the team, handles "the hard ones"
  informally. There is no queue for them; people walk to her desk.
- The head of e-commerce sponsors this engagement and wants "a number"
  for the board at the end of the quarter.
- IT has one engineer assigned half-time. Any data extract needs the
  operations manager's sign-off.

Roster you may schedule: support lead, operations manager, head of
e-commerce, the senior agent, two frontline agents (your pick of
shifts), the IT engineer, the warehouse returns supervisor.

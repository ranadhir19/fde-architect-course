# FDE 1.03 Rubric (24 points)

- Stakeholder coverage (6): every roster member scheduled or deferred
  with a reason; order justified — the people who do the work come
  before the people who sponsor it, or the plan says why not.
- Shadowing (6): at least half a day watching real work, with named
  counts (tickets touched, re-categorised, walked to the senior agent),
  not "observe the team".
- Stated vs real problem (6): at least three interview questions that
  could disprove the stated ask ("automate support") — for example,
  questions that would surface a returns-policy or category-taxonomy
  cause instead of a volume cause.
- Evidence discipline (4): every number the plan intends to quote has a
  source, a permission path (the operations manager signs off extracts),
  and a definition. The personal spreadsheet is audited, not trusted.
- Exit criteria (2): day-10 criteria include a named not-proceeding
  outcome. A plan that cannot end in "do not build" is not discovery.
- Automatic fail: any solution, tool, vendor, or architecture proposed
  in the plan.
- Pass: 18 of 24 and no automatic fail.

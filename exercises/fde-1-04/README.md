# FDE 1.04 Exercise — Turn a vague brief into a falsifiable value hypothesis

**Topic:** Problem framing and the value hypothesis (Layer 1). **Part (d) of FDE 1.04.**
Attempt this before watching the tutorial half of the lesson.

## The task

Read `vague-brief-and-findings.md`. It holds two things: the original
ask for Cedarbrook Home Goods (the fictional Capstone 1 customer from
FDE 1.03), which was simply "improve support", and the findings your
discovery work produced. The ask is not a problem statement. The
findings are not a hypothesis. Your job is to do the framing move that
connects them.

Write `value-hypothesis.md` from `value-hypothesis-template.md`. The
hypothesis must state, in order:

- The workflow, named as the people who do it experience it — not as a
  system, a tool, or a department.
- The baseline: the number as measured in discovery, with its source
  and its counting definition. Where discovery did not measure a
  number, write "unknown — measure first" and say how.
- The intervention, in one sentence, small enough to ship as a first
  slice.
- The predicted delta: the metric, the direction, the size you expect,
  and the date by which it should be visible.
- The falsifier: the observed result that would prove the hypothesis
  wrong, and what you would recommend if you saw it.
- The owner: the named role inside the customer who is paid to care
  about the metric and who accepts or rejects the result.

Then write the kill criteria for the engagement itself: the two
conditions under which you would recommend stopping, even if the
hypothesis has not yet been tested.

## Done means

- Every number in the hypothesis traces to the findings document or is
  marked unknown. Invented baselines score zero.
- The falsifier is an observation, not a feeling: a reader could check
  it against data on the stated date and agree whether it fired.
- The intervention is one workflow slice, not a programme. "Automate
  support" restated in longer words is not an intervention.
- Graded with `RUBRIC.md`. `SOLUTION.md` is the reference answer,
  released after the attempt window.

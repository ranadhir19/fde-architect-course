# FDE 1.04 Rubric (24 points)

- Workflow framing (4): the workflow is named as work people do (for
  example, answering returns questions), not as a system, tool, or
  department. Reframing "support" as one workflow earns the points;
  keeping "support" whole earns none.
- Baseline honesty (6): every number traces to the findings document
  with source and counting definition. Cost per ticket and any dollar
  figure are marked unknown, because discovery never measured them.
  One invented baseline loses all six.
- Intervention size (4): one slice, one workflow, shippable first.
  A programme, a platform, or "automate support" in longer words
  earns none.
- Predicted delta (4): metric, direction, expected size, and a date
  all present. The size is argued from the baseline (for example, a
  share of the 31% returns-question volume), not plucked from air.
- Falsifier (4): an observation a reader could check on the stated
  date, plus a recommendation for if it fires. "If it does not work
  we will iterate" earns none.
- Owner and kill criteria (2): a named customer role owns the metric,
  and both kill criteria are observable conditions, not moods.
- Automatic fail: any dollar saving stated as fact; any baseline that
  contradicts the findings (for example, quoting the 40% as-filed
  growth as real demand growth without the category correction).
- Pass: 18 of 24 and no automatic fail.

# FDE 1.02 Rubric (20 points)

- Grounding (6): every baseline traces to the brief; invented numbers score 0
  for that workflow.
- Metric quality (6): the metric is countable now and moves money, time, or
  risk — vanity metrics score 0.
- Ownership (4): a named role inside the company is paid to care.
- Ranking judgement (4): the first-ranked workflow has the clearest baseline
  and the shortest path to a measurable delta.
- Pass: 15 of 20 with no invented baseline anywhere in the submission.

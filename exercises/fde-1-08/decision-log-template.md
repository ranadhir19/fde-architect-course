# DECISIONS.md — [engagement name]

One entry per decision. Entries are never edited after the day
they are written; a changed decision gets a new entry that
supersedes the old one, and the old entry is marked SUPERSEDED
with a pointer forward. If it is not in this file, it was not
decided.

## Entry format

### D-[number] — [decision in one line] — [date]
- **Decider:** [name and role — the person with the authority]
- **Decided:** [what was committed, in the decider's terms]
- **Alternatives considered:** [what else was on the table]
- **Why:** [the reason given at the time, not a later story]
- **Status:** ACTIVE | SUPERSEDED by D-[n] | BLOCKED on [owner]
- **Cost of reversal:** [what changing this later would break]

## Raised, not decided

Questions the team discussed that no authorised decider settled.
Each entry names who can decide it and what is blocked until
they do. Anything the team is tempted to treat as settled
belongs here until it is actually decided.

## Statements that are not decisions

Promises, estimates, and opinions recorded in chat or meetings
that sound like commitments but were made without the authority
or the terms a decision needs. Recorded so nobody plans on
them by accident.

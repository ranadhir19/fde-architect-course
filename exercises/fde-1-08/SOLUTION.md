# FDE 1.08 Reference solution

Released after the attempt window. One defensible log; wording
may vary, the judgements below may not.

## The decisions

- **D-01 (Mon wk1) — Scope starts with returns questions only.**
  Decider: sponsor, confirmed in the kickoff and restated in
  chat by the support lead. Alternative: the whole support
  queue (the lead's original "automate support" ask). Status:
  ACTIVE.
- **D-02 (Tue wk1) — Walk-up ("hard ones") cases are logged on
  paper at the senior agent's desk, starting Wed wk1.** Decider:
  support lead, who owns the log. Why: the senior agent is a
  single point of failure with unmeasured volume. Status:
  ACTIVE — week 1 count 61 walk-ups, 19 of them the
  returns-window question (evidence the log itself produced).
- **D-03 (Wed wk1) — Primary metric: median handle time on
  returns tickets, baseline measured before any change goes
  live.** Decider: sponsor ("that's the metric. lock it in").
  Status: ACTIVE; baseline not yet measured.
- **D-04 (wk1, working assumption) — The draft answer path is
  written from the published policy page (30-day window).**
  Status: SUPERSEDED by D-05. This entry stays in the log:
  drafts were written against it for a week.
- **D-05 (Wed wk2) — The answer path gives 45 days for
  furniture, 30 days for everything else.** Decider: operations
  manager, in the weekly demo, on the record: the policy page
  is wrong and the spoken practice is the business's real
  promise. She signs the wording this week — signature pending
  is part of the status, not a footnote. The website fix is a
  separate e-commerce task, not part of this decision. Status:
  ACTIVE, signature pending.
- **D-06 (Thu wk1) — Legacy category renumbering is deferred
  until after v1.** Decider: support lead, FDE concurring.
  Why: no capacity to renumber mid-flight.
- **D-07 (Fri wk1) — The weekly demo moves from Friday to
  Wednesday.** Decider: support lead (sponsor's diary drove
  it). Small, and logged anyway: three people's calendars were
  planned against the old day.
- **D-08 (Wed wk2) — Board reporting date: the last Thursday
  of the quarter.** Decider: sponsor, in the room.
- **D-09 (Wed wk2) — Change freeze: no deployments to the
  returns flow during the last week of the quarter.** Source:
  the warehouse supervisor's standing rule, relayed by IT and
  confirmed in the meeting notes. Logged as a constraint with
  an owner, because the rollout plan must be built around it.
- **D-10 (Thu wk2) — The support lead's spreadsheet is the
  single source of truth for the rota; old copies are locked.**
  Decider: support lead; IT executed the locking.

## Raised, not decided

- **The order-status chatbot.** Raised by the sponsor at the
  Wed wk2 demo; general agreement it would be nice; no scope,
  date, owner, or metric discussed. Only the sponsor can put
  it in scope. Until she does, nobody plans, staffs, or dates
  it — and D-01 (returns only) remains the scope.
- **Trimming the ticket extract.** IT pulled all categories,
  not just returns, and asked whether to trim it. Nobody
  answered. Owner of the call: the FDE, with the operations
  manager's extract sign-off already given for the pull itself.

## Statements that are not decisions

- **"The triage demo will be live for the whole team by Friday
  week 2"** — said by the IT engineer in a corridor and
  repeated in chat. He is half-time, does not own the rollout,
  and a demo shown to the team is not a team going live. No
  plan may carry this date. The rollout timing is decided by
  the support lead and the FDE against D-09's freeze.

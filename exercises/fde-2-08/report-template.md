# DEMO.md — the 48-hour prototype demo plan (template)

Hand this to the ward lead before the demo. Every number
comes from your own run of `prototype.py` over
`requests.json` as delivered.

## The demo in one line

- Requests (n), total demo value (n). Prototype items
  (n), carrying value (n) — coverage as a share of the
  total. Verdict: demo-ready or not, by the stated rule.

## Built for real

- Each built request: its id, its feature, its prototype
  hours, and the one thing the live build proves.

## Faked, and labelled faked

- Each faked request: its id, its feature, what the fake
  shows, and what it does not prove. The label is part of
  the demo script, not a footnote after it.

## Deferred and cut

- Deferred: each request, with its rule — blocked, or
  safety-critical and not fakeable.
- Cut: each request, with the hours that did not fit.
- One line on what would have to change — a dependency
  landing, a longer window, a different value score from
  the ward lead — for each group to be reconsidered.

## The timebox

- Faked hours, built hours, prototype hours in total,
  and the slack left inside the sixteen hours. What the
  slack is reserved for (demo rehearsal, one failure).

## The harden list

- Every prototype item with a harden note: its id and
  the note, carried forward word for word in substance.
- The throwaway declaration: this prototype's code is
  not promoted to production; what carries forward is
  the demo record and this list.
